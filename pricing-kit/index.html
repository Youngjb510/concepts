When they push

"How much?"Answer straight, once, then stop talking. The silence after a price is theirs to fill, not yours.
"That's a lot"Compared to what? Ask it plainly. Half the time they are comparing it to nothing at all.
"Can you do it cheaper?"I can do less of it. Which part do you want to drop? Never discount, reduce scope.
"Send me a quote"I'll send it now while we're on. What's the best email? Then send it before hanging up.
"We'll think about it"Course. Is it the money, or the timing?
"Who else have you done?"Show the free clip you made them. That is the portfolio for this conversation.

Rules

  1. One product per call. A firm considering $300 does not want to hear $7,500.
  2. Lead with the free clip. It is the only thing here that costs nothing and proves everything.
  3. Take a deposit on the call. Half up front on anything over $900.
  4. Do not discount. Reduce scope instead, and say which part you removed.
  5. Every AI clip gets watched frame by frame before it goes out. On their own finished project, a wall that bends is their work rendered wrong.
