Someone choosing between three firms is deciding by eye, in about ten seconds, before they read a word of a quote.

Which means the photographs are not a record of the work. They are the work, as far as the next client is concerned. Most firms in this trade have a handful taken on a phone at the end of a job, in the middle of the day, when the light is at its worst.

The plan

What gets shot on the day.

The approach

How the garden reveals itself from the house, which is the shot that sells the design rather than the plants.

Structure

Terracing, stonework, steps and walls, in the raking light that shows the level changes.

Planting

Texture and layering close in, so the scheme reads as designed rather than as landscaping.

Water and light

Twilight on the best project. This is the set that gets saved and shared, every time.

The detail

Joints, edges and finishes. The shots that tell another designer you know what you are doing.

Timed around the light, not the calendar. That usually means an early start and staying through dusk on the best project, and it is the difference between photographs you use once and photographs you use for years.
